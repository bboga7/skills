Pages that look like someone made them.

A skill for coding agents that replaces default copy, color and motion with decisions, then checks the page and tells you what is still generic.

nakış (Turkish): hand embroidery. Each stitch is placed on purpose, never printed from a stock pattern.

What the agent does with it

  1. Reads the brief before it writes code.Page type, reader, one main action, three tone words. It asks one question when the brief is unclear.
  2. Writes the copy first, in English or Turkish.Stock phrases, mirror sentences and translation smell are rewritten into specific claims. Every claim has to come from the brief; anything else is left out or flagged for the owner.
  3. Builds a palette from one seed color.Neutrals are tinted, text is checked against its background, and the accent stays rare.
  4. Adds one moment of motion.A headline that unveils, a thread that draws itself. Everything stops when the reader asks for less motion.
  5. Audits the result.A local script scores the page from 0 to 100 and lists each pattern it found, with the line to fix.

Install in two steps

Copy the nakis folder into the skills folder of your agent. Then ask it for a page, or for a review of one you already have. To check a page yourself:

python3 nakis/scripts/audit.py path/to/index.html
python3 nakis/scripts/palette.py "#9a3412"

Runs on your machine

No servers, no keys, no font services, no model calls inside the scripts. Plain files you can read in an afternoon.
